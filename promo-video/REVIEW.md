# Review: "The number that comes twice" (render of 2026-11-15)

Delivered: `renders/bank-data-promo.mp4` (gitignored), copied to `docs/video/bank-data-promo.mp4`
and embedded on `docs/index.html`. H.264, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 33.5 s, 4.7 MB, silent.

## Creative calls
- Built storyline A (STORYLINES.md) without a pick round, because the request was for the
  finished file. B ("Plate I", a flight across the map) and C ("Footnotes", the page's notes as
  kinetic type) are written up but not built.
- Silent on purpose: the film plays muted on the page, and music or a voice would need a HeyGen
  sign-in (the person's step) or local tools nobody asked for. Captions carry the narration;
  titles carry the figures and the page's own copy.
- The map is the page's real SVG (generated by `tools/build_plate.py`, so it can be rebuilt from
  the page); the dashboard is its three real screenshots from `docs/img/`. No UI was redrawn.
- The plate settles at 0.86 scale so no marker sits under the caption band.

## Measured vs looked at
- `hyperframes check`: 0 errors. Kept on purpose: `film_silent` (see above); `text_below_floor`
  on the 28 map numerals (11-15 px at 1080p): they're the plate's texture, and the captions say
  "28 banks"; `escaped_container` on `#ds-cam` (the deliberate 1.9x punch-in).
- Looked at: snapshots at every SPEC QA time, plus a tile of decoded frames from the MP4 itself.
  ffprobe duration 33.500 s = root `data-duration`.
- Not checked: playback on real phones and Safari (H.264 / yuv420p should play everywhere).

## Weakest shot and what I'd change with more time
- 18.7-21.7 s, the concept picker: the screenshot's text is about 14 px at 1080p, so it reads as
  "a form with ticks", not as the concept names. With a fresh capture I'd rebuild just the
  Profitability and Asset-quality panels at 1.8x and tick the boxes on screen.
- The dashboard beats are stills with camera moves. A real screen recording of the basket being
  filled would prove the interaction better.
