# Storylines: Bank_Data promo (~33 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps)

The one thing: Bank_Data puts European bank figures on a map and traces every number to the
filing it came from. When two filings disagree, it shows both.
What a stranger should remember: "click banks on a map, compare them, every number shows where it came from."
The one real piece of data that travels through the film: Erste Group's total risk exposure,
EU OV1 149.7bn vs EU KM1 147.5bn (Raiffeisen: 101.3bn vs 100.9bn). All copy and figures are
taken from docs/index.html.

## A. "The number that comes twice" (recommended, built)

Recommended because a stranger gets the point from one real number before any interface appears,
and it's the project's real differentiator, not a feature list.

- Device: a number lands. Form: carry across (the disputed figure shrinks into the amber ring on
  the map, the map becomes the dashboard).
- Sees and feels: one bank figure, then a second, different figure for the same thing from the
  same bank. Who's right? The film pulls back to the atlas plate of Europe, then into the dashboard,
  where both figures sit side by side in amber.
- First 2 s: white plate, "Erste Group, total risk exposure, FY2025", then 149.7 lands, tagged EU OV1.
- Beats: hook (149.7) → tension (147.5, 1.5% apart; Raiffeisen too) → reveal (zoom out from Austria's
  amber ring to the 28-bank plate, headline) → proof (dashboard: basket, concepts, table) → payoff
  (punch-in on the amber RWA row) → claim on plate green → end card with the button.
- Look: the page's Atlas Plate I: white paper, near-black ink, graded greens, amber only for
  disagreement, Archivo.
- Sound: silent by design (it plays muted on the page); titles and captions carry the words.

## B. "Plate I" (not built)

- Device: the object is the explanation. Form: a flight through one world. The camera travels
  across the atlas plate, stopping at each country cluster as its key entries write themselves in,
  and ending on Austria's ring and the two figures.
- Fails the stranger test slightly: the "why" (disagreement) only arrives at the end.

## C. "Footnotes" (not built, the bold one)

- Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds. The film is the page's five
  plate notes set as giant atlas footnotes, each superscript numeral opening the next note,
  screenshots appearing as figure plates.
- Risk: reads as a slideshow of text; strongest for an audience that already reads the page.
