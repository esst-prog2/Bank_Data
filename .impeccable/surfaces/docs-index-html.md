## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Plate I of a statistical atlas of European banking; the map plate is the hero and the argument. It refuses the centred-headline-over-screenshot-and-three-cards project page.

OWN-WORLD: White plate paper, near-black ink #141413, land in graded greens #cdeadd to #0f5a3c by bank count, deep green #0f5a3c for neatline, title cartouche and the primary action; one amber only for source disagreement, matching the dashboard (#8a5a00 text, #fff8ec wash, #d9a441 rule, #b07410 map ring). Archivo across widths: expanded tracked caps as map lettering, normal width for notes, tabular figures. Double-rule neatline with graduated tick border; numbered markers; legend box; superscript note calls.

STORY: Visitor sees Europe with 28 numbered banks, reads that every figure is traced to its filing, sees one bank whose two sources disagree kept as two numbers, and opens the dashboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width neatline plate. Map fills it; title cartouche in the Atlantic, top-left, with hook, one-line explanation and the primary button "Open the interactive plate". Key (28 numbered banks by country) in the right margin column inside the neatline. Legend bottom-left. Erste and RBI markers ringed amber with a leader to a callout showing 149.7bn | 147.5bn.
